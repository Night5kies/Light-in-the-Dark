# Light in the Dark (3D RPG)

**Light in the Dark** is a story-driven 3D RPG built in **Unity (C#)** by a 10-person team.  
The game blends combat, exploration, and narrative to immerse players in a world where the balance of nature is under threat. Players fight to protect the environment from corruption while uncovering a deeper story about restoration and responsibility.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🌍 **Story of Environmental Protection** – a narrative focused on restoring balance to a corrupted world and safeguarding nature  
- ⚔️ **Combat System** – modular melee/ranged mechanics with event-driven interactions  
- 💬 **Dialogue & Cutscenes** – branching dialogue trees and cinematic cutscenes that bring the environmental themes to life  
- 🎭 **Animation State Machines** – fluid transitions for character movement, combat, and exploration  
- 👥 **Team Collaboration** – built by a 10-person cross-functional team across design, audio, art, and programming
